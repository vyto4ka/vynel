Our story

Started with one roaster in a garage.

In 2017 we bought a 5 kg drum roaster and a lot of green coffee we did not yet know how to roast. Today we roast two tonnes a month and still taste every batch.

We buy directly or through importers who publish what they pay farmers. Every bag lists the farm, the price we paid per kilo and the roast date.
